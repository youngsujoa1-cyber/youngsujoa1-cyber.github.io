1. 총칙

구름 날씨 레이더(이하 “앱”)는 기상청 레이더 합성영상을 지도 위에서 재생하고, 예보·미세먼지를 함께 보여 주는 날씨 앱입니다. 개발자 Topster(이하 “개발자”)는 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 여기며, 관련 법령과 Google Play 정책을 준수합니다. 본 방침은 앱이 어떤 정보를 다루고, 그 정보가 어디에 저장되며, 기기 밖으로 무엇이 오가는지 설명합니다.

핵심 요약: 앱은 회원가입·로그인이 없고, 개발자가 운영하는 서버도 없습니다. 앱이 저장하는 것은 이용자가 고른 지역과 앱 설정뿐이며, 이는 기기의 앱 전용 영역에만 있습니다. 날씨·미세먼지·레이더 자료를 받기 위해 지역 좌표와 공공데이터 서비스 키가 해당 공공 API로 전송되고, 지도·위성 타일을 받기 위해 타일 좌표가 지도 타일 서버로 전송됩니다. 그 밖의 개인정보를 수집하거나 판매하지 않습니다.

앱은 한국어·영어·일본어·스페인어·중국어 5개 언어로 제공되며 Google Play 스토어에서 배포됩니다. 본 방침은 한국어와 영어로 제공되고, 어느 나라에서 앱을 내려받았든 같은 내용이 적용됩니다.

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

구름 날씨 레이더 (“the App”) is a weather app that plays the Korea Meteorological Administration’s radar composite imagery on a map and shows forecasts and air quality together. The developer, Topster (“the Developer”), respects your privacy and follows applicable law and Google Play policies. This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored, and what leaves your device.

In short: The App has no sign-up, no login, and no server run by the Developer. What it stores is the region you chose and your app settings, kept only in the App’s private area on your device. To fetch weather, air quality and radar data, the region’s coordinates and a public-data service key are sent to those public APIs, and tile coordinates are sent to map and satellite tile servers. No other personal information is collected or sold.

The App is available in five languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish and Chinese) and is distributed through Google Play. This policy is provided in Korean and English and applies to users in every country where the App is downloaded.

2. Information the App stores (on your device)

The following is stored only in the App’s private area on your device and is not sent to any Developer server.

Chosen region The name and coordinates of the neighbourhood you picked, or the coordinates of the location found with “My location”, kept so the same region opens next time.
Alert settings Whether rain alerts are on, check interval, alert radius, and the time the last alert was sent
Public-data service key The KMA API Hub service key you obtained and entered yourself (if any). It is stored in App settings and used in weather and air-quality requests.
App settings App language, temperature and wind units, light map on/off, whether the first-run guide was completed
Cache Weather responses, map/radar/satellite tile images, and radar photos or videos made for sharing. Android may clear it to free space, and it is removed with the App.

The App keeps no location history. “My location” reads your position once to choose a region, and only that region’s coordinates are stored as the “chosen region”. No route or list of past locations is kept.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and displays ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support its operation.

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), device information, IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • That data is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. The Developer does not receive information from AdMob that identifies individual users.

Apart from this advertising SDK, the App uses no analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no server of its own.

6. Sharing features

  • When you share a radar photo, video or forecast card, the App hands that one file plus an app-link caption to the app you choose (KakaoTalk, mail and so on). Storage and transmission after that follow that app’s privacy policy.
  • Sharing also copies the app link to the clipboard. The clipboard is used only on your device and is not sent anywhere.
  • The promo banner is drawn into images the App creates and contains no information about you.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Region, settings, key and cache are stored on your device. The Developer keeps none of them.
  • You can change the region or clear the cache in settings, and uninstalling the App removes the settings and cache stored on the device.
  • Copies of photos or videos you shared remain in the app you sent them to (KakaoTalk, mail and so on); delete them there if needed.
  • The Developer keeps no personal data separately, so there is nothing to delete. Support emails you send are used only to answer your inquiry and are deleted promptly afterwards.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can grant or revoke the location permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → 구름 날씨 레이더 → Permissions. Without it, pick a region from the list instead of “My location”.
  • You can turn rain alerts off in the App’s settings and revoke the notification permission in Android Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in Android Settings.
  • Because the Developer holds no personal data on a server, any access, correction or deletion is done on the data in your own device, which the App lets you manage directly. If you need help or have another question, contact us at the email below.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Security

Region, settings and cache are kept in the App’s private storage area, which other apps cannot readily access. All traffic the App sends and receives is encrypted over HTTPS. Because the Developer keeps no user information on a server, a server breach cannot expose it.
